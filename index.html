We tell you when to leave Bubble

If your roadmap needs self-hosted infrastructure, sub-second latency or a 40-person engineering org, we will say so on the first call — and point you to people who can help.

You keep the keys

Every project is handed over in your editor, with documented data structures, named workflows and a recorded walkthrough. No hostage situations.

No-code is not no craft

Anyone can build a page. The difference is in the data model, the conditional logic, the edge cases and the load times. That is where we work.

How we work

A process built for
shipping, not billable hours.

  1. 01

    Scoping & architecture

    Two weeks. We map your flows, define the data model, flag the risky parts of Bubble and hand you a fixed-scope build plan.

    Week 1–2
  2. 02

    Design system

    One to two weeks. Figma components, responsive rules and states — designed to be rebuilt cleanly as Bubble reusable elements.

    Week 2–4
  3. 03

    Bubble build

    Two to four weeks. We build in your editor daily, in a shared project, with a live staging link from day one.

    Week 4–7
  4. 04

    QA & launch

    One week. Edge cases, permissions, mobile, payments, email, performance passes. Then we ship and monitor.

    Week 7–8
  5. 05

    Iterate & scale

    Ongoing. A weekly retainer, a backlog tied to revenue, and architecture that keeps up as your user base grows.

    Month 2+
